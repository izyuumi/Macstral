# Macstral — Refund Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-01_

We want you to be happy with **Macstral Pro**. Because the free, on-device tier
lets you fully evaluate Macstral's core dictation before paying, and Pro is a
one-time purchase, this policy applies to the **$30 Pro unlock**.

## 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with Macstral Pro, you may request a full refund within
**14 days** of purchase, for any reason. No justification is required.

## How to Request a Refund

Email **mail@yumi.to** from the address used at purchase, or include your Lemon
Squeezy order number. You can also reply to your Lemon Squeezy receipt. We aim to
respond within **3 business days**.

## Processing

- Purchases and refunds are handled by **Lemon Squeezy**, our **merchant of
  record**. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method by Lemon
  Squeezy and typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank
  or card provider.
- On refund, the associated Pro license key is deactivated and Pro features are
  disabled. The free, on-device tier remains available.

## Exceptions

We may decline refunds in cases of abuse — for example, repeated purchase-and-
refund cycles, or evidence of license-key sharing, resale, or circumvention.
